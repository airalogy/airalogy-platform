# Project meetings and research activity

Open Meetings in a Project to record a completed meeting. Enter its actual date; exact time is optional. Backdated minutes remain on the occurrence date. You may prepare an agenda before the meeting, with an optional scheduled time. Planned and cancelled meetings are excluded from occurred activity.

## Participants and access

Select project accounts or record external participants by name and organization. Attendance distinguishes attended, invited, absent and unconfirmed. Attendance does not grant access. Project readers are the default audience; selected-member meetings require explicit viewer/commenter/editor grants. The author retains access and manages people and audience. Underlying project permissions are rechecked, and no membership or guest invitation is created. Audience applies to minutes and their history.

Meeting lists, document reads/history/browsing, activity counts and work evidence enforce the same meeting access. Minutes share one document and revision history. Attachments retain their own scope: meeting access does not make a project file participant-only or grant access to an otherwise private file. External-account invitations and guest sharing are not part of this delivery.

## Minutes, comments and actions

Record discussion, decisions/rationale, open questions and next steps. Save and check the feedback. Conflicting revisions are rejected with input retained; compare the latest version before merging. Leaving with unsaved input prompts a warning. Comments are appended with author/time, independently of the minutes.

After saving, link existing work or create an action with optional assignee and planned completion. Work follows project work permissions and links the exact meeting revision; the minutes body is not copied. Project readers without meeting access can follow the work but cannot open the meeting source. Retried creation requests do not duplicate work. Live work state and historical minutes remain distinct; later meetings reference the same work identity.

## Research activity

Project Overview displays a recent 90-day heatmap and activity list. Select a day for its detail, or clear it to return to recent activity. Meeting/document/Record/research/Protocol/Knowledge filters apply to both counts and detail. The full activity view provides year selection and pagination.

A meeting is counted once on its actual occurrence date. Editing minutes or attendance does not add activity. Date boundaries and counts share the browser timezone, available from the information button beside the date range; unreadable activity is excluded before aggregation. Counts support recall, not productivity scoring. Existing documents, work, Protocols and Records retain their own save/submission events; ordinary comments and title edits are not work progress.

Recurring schedules, reminders, real-time text collaboration, transcription and AI summaries remain future work. Ordinary meetings require no Protocol; structured reviews/tests may link existing Protocols and Records.
